# Amazon Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Amazon sales performance, customer behavior, and discount impact.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
**Key Features**

- Executive overview with revenue, profit, and order KPIs
- Deep analysis of discounts, customer ratings, and reviews
- Interactive filters for dynamic exploration
- Scatter plots to analyze relationships between revenue, discounts, and ratings

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Key Insights from the Analysis:

• Revenue increased from 2022 to 2023, showing a positive sales trend.
• Electronics and Fashion categories are major contributors to overall revenue.
• Higher customer ratings tend to align with higher revenue.
• Products with more reviews generally perform better in terms of sales.
• Discounts influence both revenue and profit, highlighting the importance of balanced pricing strategies.
